GET TO KNOW THE CANDIDATE

About Comrade Aminu Bala Tafida

A son of Mayo-Belwa with a passion for advocacy, community development and meaningful representation.

My name is Comrade Aminu Bala Tafida. I am a 29-year-old Engineering graduate, born and brought up in Mayo-Belwa, Adamawa State.

Over the years, I have had the privilege of working with several reputable Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs), where I gained practical experience in community development, humanitarian services and working with people from all walks of life. These experiences opened my eyes to the real challenges our people face daily at the grassroots.

Since childhood, I have always had a passion for advocacy: speaking up for the voiceless, listening to the concerns of ordinary people, and supporting activities that bring positive change to society.

My decision to contest for the Mayo-Belwa Constituency seat in the Adamawa State House of Assembly in the 2027 General Election under the Young Progressives Party (YPP) is not for personal ambition. It is driven by a deep desire to serve my people and contribute to the development of our beloved constituency.

I believe the people deserve a representative who listens, understands their needs and remains connected to them. To me, leadership is about service, commitment and accountability to the people.

This is an opportunity for me to turn my passion for advocacy and community service into greater responsibility and meaningful representation.

THE PEOPLE'S AGENDA 2027

A New Vision for Mayo-Belwa

Unity. Service. Accountability. A practical agenda focused on the needs of our communities and the future of our people.

Education

Championing quality education and better opportunities for every child.

  • Press for renovation and furniture in public schools.
  • Advocate for teacher training and welfare.
  • Support education assistance for indigent students.
  • Promote girl-child education.
  • Support literacy programmes and basic ICT access.

Youth, Women Empowerment & Employment

Creating opportunities and supporting the economic participation of our people.

  • Connect youth and women to loan and grant schemes.
  • Advocate for local employment opportunities.
  • Promote women's participation in community decisions.
  • Push for better facilities at Mayo-Belwa Friday Market.

Skills Acquisition

Equipping young people with practical skills that can lead to sustainable income.

  • Training in tailoring, welding and phone repairs.
  • Basic computer and digital skills training.
  • Apprenticeship opportunities with local craftsmen.
  • Support access to start-up tools as resources allow.
  • Training in solar installation and farm processing.

Agriculture

Supporting farmers, livestock owners and agricultural development.

  • Advocate for fair distribution of seeds and fertiliser.
  • Promote irrigation and borehole access.
  • Support storage and processing of farm produce.
  • Push for improved feeder roads.
  • Support farmer and herder cooperatives.
  • Advocate for veterinary services and livestock vaccination.

Peace, Security & Social Cohesion

Building peaceful communities where everyone can live and work together.

  • Promote dialogue between farmers and herders.
  • Support community early-warning systems.
  • Champion equal treatment across communities.
  • Advocate for displaced families and affected children.

Good Governance & Community Development

Promoting transparent representation and accountability to the people.

  • Scrutinise state budgets and advocate for Mayo-Belwa.
  • Maintain a functional constituency office.
  • Track roads, water and electricity projects.
  • Publish public reports on constituency work twice yearly.
  • Include youth, women, elders and persons with disabilities.

Healthcare & Well-being

Advocating for accessible and functional healthcare services in our communities.

  • Push for renovation and staffing of primary health centres.
  • Support maternal and child healthcare.
  • Promote access to health insurance schemes.
  • Advocate for emergency referral services.
  • Support health awareness and outreach programmes.
